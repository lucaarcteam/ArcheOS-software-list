# DCMTK

Back to the [DCMTK entry](../dicom.md) in the software list.

This page collects the extra information of the entry: bibliography, webography and field use.

## Bibliography

No published archaeological attestation citing DCMTK verified to date: the archaeological use of the toolkit by Arc-Team is documented on ATOR (see the webography below). The publication of the project in which the toolkit was adopted — the 3D documentation of Ötzi (Heritage, 2026) — does not cite DCMTK by name.

**Reference bibliography** (tool, developer — context, not an attestation): M. Eichelberg, J. Riesmeier, T. Wilkens, A. J. Hewett, A. Barth, P. Jensch (2004), *Ten years of medical imaging standardization and prototypical implementation: the DICOM standard and the OFFIS DICOM toolkit (DCMTK)*, Proceedings of SPIE Medical Imaging 2004: PACS and Imaging Informatics, 5371, pp. 57–68.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.534853): the standard reference of the toolkit by its developers (OFFIS); context.

## Webography

**First use online**: *DCMTK, a collection of libraries for DICOM standard* (ATOR, 11 May 2026), where Alessandro Bezzi documents the use of DCMTK to extract the metadata of the DICOM data of the CT scan of Ötzi (performed in 2021 by the South Tyrol Health Care Service) during the 2022–2024 project of the 3D documentation of the mummy.
<https://arc-team-open-research.blogspot.com/2026/05/dcmtk-collection-of-libraries-for-dicom.html>

**Later**: *Preserving the Past: Ötzi's Digital Twin* (ATOR, 12 September 2026), announcement of the publication of the Heritage article, recalling the analysis of the CT DICOM data performed with the open-source DCMTK libraries.
<https://arc-team-open-research.blogspot.com/2026/09/preserving-past-otzis-digital-twin.html>

## In field use since

2022, first documented in the ATOR article on DCMTK (11 May 2026), which describes the use of the toolkit for the extraction of the metadata of the DICOM data of the 2021 CT scan of Ötzi during the 2022–2024 3D documentation project.
