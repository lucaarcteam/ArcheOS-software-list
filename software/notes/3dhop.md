# 3DHOP

Back to the [3DHOP entry](../outreach_3d_models.md) in the software list.

This page collects the extra information of the entry: bibliography, webography and field use.

## Bibliography

**First archaeological use** (Arc-Team): L. Bezzi, A. Bezzi, C. Boscaro, K. Feistmantl, R. Gietl, G. Naponiello, F. Ottati, M. de Guzman (2018), *Commercial Archaeology and 3D Web Technologies*, Journal of Field Archaeology 43(sup1), pp. S45–S59.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/00934690.2018.1505410): the team chose 3DHOP for the online publication of the 3D models of a commercial archaeology project.

**Later** (third party): P. Derudas, F. Nurra, A. Svensson (2023), *New AIR for the Archaeological Process? The Use of 3D Web Semantic for Publishing Archaeological Reports*, Journal on Computing and Cultural Heritage 16(3), pp. 1–23.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3594722): the online publication of the archaeological excavation data is achieved with 3DHOP.

**Founding paper** (developers, context): M. Potenziani, M. Callieri, M. Dellepiane, M. Corsini, F. Ponchio, R. Scopigno (2015), *3DHOP: 3D Heritage Online Presenter*, Computers & Graphics 52, pp. 129–141.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cag.2015.07.001): the presentation of the framework by the CNR-ISTI Visual Computing Lab; context for the attestations above.

## Webography

**First use online**: *ArcheOS Hypatia 3DHOP package (call for testing)* (ATOR, 26 December 2015), showing a 3D web-gallery made with 3DHOP for the WW1 conflict archaeology project.
<https://arc-team-open-research.blogspot.com/2015/12/archeos-hypatia-3dhop-package-call-for.html>

**Later**: *3DHOP for speleoarchaeology* (ATOR, 27 March 2020), DTM and 3D reconstruction of the Bus dela Spia cave presented with the slicer tool.
<https://arc-team-open-research.blogspot.com/2020/03/3dhop-for-speleoarchaeology.html>

*3DHOP X-Ray Tool* (ATOR, 3 May 2026), a 3DHOP extension to view the Ötzi mummy in X-ray mode.
<https://arc-team-open-research.blogspot.com/2026/05/3dhop-x-ray-tool.html>

*Preserving the Past: Ötzi's Digital Twin* (ATOR, 12 September 2026), the article on the mummy's digital twin built on the 3DHOP X-ray workflow.
<https://arc-team-open-research.blogspot.com/2026/09/preserving-past-otzis-digital-twin.html>

## In field use since

2015, first documented in the ATOR article on the ArcheOS 3DHOP package, showing the WW1 conflict-archaeology 3D web-gallery (26 December 2015).
